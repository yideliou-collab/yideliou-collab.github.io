Work with us

We welcome students and collaborators who connect careful experiments with testable physical models. The AFM-based nanoscale characterization and microwave-enabled semiconductor characterization platforms are being established.

  • Explore ferroic textures with scanning-probe measurements and quantitative analysis.
  • Build phase-field models that connect free energy, boundary conditions, and dynamics.
  • Connect ultrafast optical response to local structure and coupled physical mechanisms.
