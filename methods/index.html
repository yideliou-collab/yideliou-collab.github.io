Phase-field & multiphysics modeling

Computational methods

Landau–Ginzburg–Devonshire and phase-field models connect local states to functional response. Coupled electrostatics, elasticity, flexoelectricity, and dynamics provide testable mechanisms alongside measurements.

How we work

Nanoscale characterization

AFM, piezoresponse force microscopy, local mechanical response, and surface-potential measurements provide complementary observables; polarization, charge, and strain interpretations require calibration and boundary-aware analysis.

Collaborative ultrafast spectroscopy

Collaborative pump–probe spectroscopy probes ensemble dynamics following optical excitation. Optical response, mode symmetry, and coupled dynamical models help distinguish plausible carrier, thermal, elastic, and order-parameter pathways.

Computational methods

Landau–Ginzburg–Devonshire theory, phase-field modeling, electrostatics, elasticity, carrier dynamics, and Dedalus/Python workflows are used to form testable predictions.
