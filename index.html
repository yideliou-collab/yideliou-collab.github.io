From local states to collective dynamics

Mesoscopic Imaging and Nonequilibrium Dynamics Laboratory

Condensed Matter & Interface Physics · NYCU

We investigate how local manipulation shapes functional responses in quantum materials and complex oxides. Scanning-probe experiments and phase-field modeling connect local states, interfaces, and defects to collective dynamics.

Research at a glance

1. Manipulation & characterization

How can local manipulation reshape quantum materials and control their functional responses?

2. Polar configurations

How do geometry, interfaces, defects, and screening stabilize local polar states?

3. Ultrafast collective dynamics

How do photoexcited carriers and coupled order parameters drive collective dynamics? We test mechanisms with phase-field models.
