Connecting photoexcited carriers to ferroelectric switching

2021 · Research highlight

Rapid, nonvolatile optical control of mixed-phase BiFeO3

Short and ultrashort light pulses can alter the phases and ferroic orders of mixed-phase BiFeO3 thin films, with the changes persisting after illumination. Light-induced transient strain is identified as a key factor in determining the final state. Sequential pulses further enable large-area manipulation of phases and domain structures. Published in Advanced Materials.
